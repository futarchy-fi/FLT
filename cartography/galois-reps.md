# Galois representations attached to automorphic forms (hub-lsb1u.10)

## Executive conclusion

The repo’s route specification is Carayol/Taylor: the automorphic-representation file says that the attachment is “by the construction of Carayol, Taylor et al.” [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/GaloisRepresentation/Automorphic.lean:20-26`].  The blueprint introduction likewise says FLT assumes Galois representations attached to weight-2 Hilbert modular forms [`/Users/kas/FLT/blueprint/src/chapter/ch01introduction.tex:49-52`].  The blueprint mentions Shimura varieties as future algebraic-geometry infrastructure [`/Users/kas/FLT/blueprint/src/chapter/chtopbestiary.tex:247-250`], but the attachment hit itself is the Carayol/Taylor one, not an Eichler–Shimura statement.

**Weakest sufficient statement.**  For a totally real field `F` of even degree, a discriminant-1/totally-definite quaternion algebra `D/F` unramified at finite places, and a weight-2 trivial-character square-free `U₁(S)` eigenform, attach a 2-dimensional p-adic representation (and the mod p specializations used by the campaign).  This is exactly the specialization the Lean file calls “far more restrictive ... [but] suffice for ... FLT” [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/GaloisRepresentation/Automorphic.lean:56-64`]; its notes say weight 2 and everywhere-finite-unramified `D` are the only cases needed [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/GaloisRepresentation/Automorphic.lean:30-35`].

So the answer to the scope choice is **weight 2 only**, not a general-weight attachment; the answer to the compatibility choice is **Frobenius/Hecke data at good unramified primes only**, not full local Langlands at bad places [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/GaloisRepresentation/Automorphic.lean:30-35`, `/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/GaloisRepresentation/Automorphic.lean:86-95`].

The required compatibility is **good-primes only**: for every finite `v ∉ S` with `v ∤ p`, the representation is unramified, 
\(\det\rho(\mathrm{Frob}_v)=N(v)\), and \(\operatorname{tr}\rho(\mathrm{Frob}_v)\) is the `T_v` eigenvalue [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/GaloisRepresentation/Automorphic.lean:86-95`].  Equivalently, in the repo’s compatible-family API, the characteristic polynomial of arithmetic Frobenius is the common Hecke polynomial at such `v` [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/Deformations/RepresentationTheory/GaloisRepFamily.lean:47-64`].  No full local-Langlands/Weil–Deligne matching at `v ∈ S` is required by this predicate: the quantified attachment equations range only over `v ∉ S` [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/GaloisRepresentation/Automorphic.lean:86-95`].  Bad-place tameness, flatness at `p`, and cyclotomic determinant are separate deformation/modularity hypotheses, not extra bad-place local-global compatibility [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/GaloisRepresentation/Automorphic.lean:117-122`].

## 1. Standard chapter map: what FLT needs

1. **Quaternionic weight-2 eigenforms and Hecke algebra.**  Use the existing spaces of weight-2 forms on totally definite quaternion algebras and their `U₁(S,Q)` Hecke algebra.  The form space is explicitly weight 2 [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/AutomorphicForm/QuaternionAlgebra/Basic.lean:26-34`], and the concrete Hecke development supplies `T_v` at good places, `U_v` at bad places, and the generated Hecke algebra [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/AutomorphicForm/QuaternionAlgebra/HeckeOperators/Concrete.lean:38-57`].
2. **Carayol/Taylor attachment.**  State the existence of the 2-dimensional p-adic (and mod p) Galois representation associated with a permitted eigenform.  The assumptions directory lists precisely “the existence of a p-adic Galois representation attached to a weight 2 automorphic form over a totally definite quaternion algebra” as forthcoming [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/Assumptions/README.md:42-50`].
3. **Good-prime local-global compatibility.**  At `v ∉ S`, `v ∤ p`, require unramifiedness, determinant `N(v)`, and trace equal to the `T_v` eigenvalue; package this as a degree-2 Frobenius characteristic polynomial.  These are the exact equations in `IsAutomorphicOfLevel` [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/GaloisRepresentation/Automorphic.lean:80-95`].
4. **Compatible family across coefficient places.**  For the later “switch to 3” argument, provide a family ρλ with a finite bad set `S` and common `P_v`, with Frobenius characteristic-polynomial identities only when `v` and λ lie over different rational primes.  The blueprint says the campaign puts a potentially modular representation into an ℓ-adic family and then specializes at 3 [`/Users/kas/FLT/blueprint/src/chapter/ch04overview.tex:100-108`].  The API calls this “the weakest possible concept ... [that] will suffice for our needs” [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/Deformations/RepresentationTheory/GaloisRepFamily.lean:47-56`], and the blueprint’s corresponding theorem is a compatible family attached to a weight-2 inner-form representation [`/Users/kas/FLT/blueprint/src/chapter/chtopbestiary.tex:218-229`].
5. **Integral/mod-π specialization interface.**  Relate the characteristic-zero representation to the mod-π representation and preserve the good-prime Frobenius data.  This is needed because the automorphic definition explicitly covers both p-adic and mod p representations [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/GaloisRepresentation/Automorphic.lean:20-26`], while the modularity-lifting theorem compares a modular residual representation with an S-good lift [`/Users/kas/FLT/blueprint/src/chapter/ch04overview.tex:62-76`].  The actual Carayol/Taylor specialization theorem is literature input (literature-verify).
6. **Separate local deformation hypotheses.**  Record (without upgrading them to local Langlands compatibility) unramifiedness outside `S ∪ {p}`, flatness at places above `p`, cyclotomic determinant, and a tame rank-one quotient at `S`; these are the hypotheses written into the cyclic-base-change/modularity-lifting interface [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/GaloisRepresentation/Automorphic.lean:109-125`, `/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/GaloisRepresentation/Automorphic.lean:164-178`, `/Users/kas/FLT/blueprint/src/chapter/ch04overview.tex:49-60`].
7. **JL/base-change transport.**  Supply the Jacquet–Langlands and cyclic-base-change results that move between Hilbert/`GL₂` language and the quaternionic predicate.  The blueprint lists Jacquet–Langlands among the potential-modularity inputs [`/Users/kas/FLT/blueprint/src/chapter/ch04overview.tex:21-33`] and says the minimal argument needs cyclic base change and Jacquet–Langlands [`/Users/kas/FLT/blueprint/src/chapter/ch04overview.tex:84-90`]; the Lean file states the cyclic-base-change automorphy equivalence [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/GaloisRepresentation/Automorphic.lean:109-125`].  The published transfer theorems are literature input (literature-verify).
8. **Hecke-to-R=T bridge.**  Use the trace/`T_v` equations to define the Hecke-side trace/deformation map consumed by Taylor–Wiles patching (the pseudorepresentation packaging is literature-verify).  The attachment predicate already takes a Hecke-algebra eigenmap and equates Frobenius traces with `T_v` [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/GaloisRepresentation/Automorphic.lean:80-95`]; the repo’s `R = T` file describes the final patching step as the kernel/nilradical comparison making `R → T` an isomorphism after reduction [`/Users/kas/FLT/FLT/Patching/REqualsT.lean:10-15`].
