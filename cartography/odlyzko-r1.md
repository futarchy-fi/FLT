## 0. Summary

The consumer-side bound is: **the number field `K` cut out by an
irreducible mod-3 hardly-ramified representation has root discriminant
`rd(K) < 2^(2/3) · 3^(3/2) = 8.248377821991616…`**, with the `3^(3/2)`
factor coming from Fontaine's flat-group-scheme different bound
(`n + 1/(p−1)` with `p = 3`, `n = 1`) and the `2^(2/3)` factor from
tameness at 2 with ramification index dividing 3. This sits strictly
below the axiom's `8.25` (margin `0.0016222`, i.e. `0.0197 %`) and far
below Poitou's GRH-free `9.3057` at degree 18 (margin `12.8 %`). The
widely-assumed value **8.2484 is CONFIRMED, now with a primary-source
derivation** rather than an unsourced import.

Primary citation, verified against the published scan:
**J.-M. Fontaine, "Il n'y a pas de variété abélienne sur Z",
Invent. Math. 81 (1985), 515–538; Théorème A (p. 515) and its Corollaire
(p. 516).** PDF of the Springer printing hosted by the author:
`https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/~fontaine/varabZ.pdf`
(DOI 10.1007/BF01388584). Both statements were read verbatim off pages
515–516 of that scan on 2026-08-14.

## 1. The bound, derived

### 1.1 Which field

Let `k` be a finite field of characteristic 3 and
`ρ̄ : G_ℚ → GL(V) ≅ GL₂(k)` a **hardly ramified** representation
(blueprint Definition `hardly_ramified`, ch03freyreduction.tex lines
76–95; Lean `GaloisRepresentation.IsHardlyRamified`): det = mod-3
cyclotomic, unramified outside 6, at 2 reducible with unramified
square-trivial quotient, flat at 3. Assume (for contradiction, as in
`mod_three` / blueprint `hardly_ramified_mod3_reducible`) that `ρ̄` is
irreducible.

**The field is `K = fixed field of ker ρ̄` — the kernel of the actual
representation, NOT of its projectivization** (panel watch item R5: the
projective kernel field would break the flatness link used below and
would have the wrong degree/complex-conjugation bookkeeping). `K/ℚ` is
Galois with `Gal(K/ℚ) ≅ im ρ̄`, ramified only inside {2, 3}. It is
totally complex: `det ρ̄(c) = χ̄₃(c) = −1 ≠ 1` in `k` (char 3), so
complex conjugation is nontrivial in `Gal(K/ℚ)` and, being a normal
extension with one complex embedding, `K` has no real embedding.
[DERIVED — standard]

### 1.2 Ramification at 3 (Fontaine)

Verbatim from the primary source (Fontaine 1985, pp. 515–516; `K` there
is a complete discretely-valued field of characteristic 0 with perfect
residue field of characteristic `p`, `e` its absolute ramification
index, `G^(u)` the upper-numbering ramification groups in Serre's
sense):

> **Théorème A.** *Soit `n` un entier ≥ 1 et soit `J` un groupe fini sur
> l'anneau des entiers `𝔒_K` de `K`, tué par `pⁿ`. Si
> `u > e(n + 1/(p−1))`, `G^(u)` opère trivialement sur `J(K̄)`.*
>
> **Corollaire.** [`L` = the subfield of `K̄` generated by the points of
> `J`] *Soit `v₀` la valuation de `L` normalisée par `v₀(p) = 1`. Alors*
> `v₀(𝔇_{L/K}) < n + 1/(p−1)`.

[VERIFIED-with-URL:
https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/~fontaine/varabZ.pdf,
pp. 515–516; also announced in §0.2 there, where Fontaine explicitly
says the corollary gives a majoration of `|d_F|^{1/m}` to be compared
with the "minorations fournies par la méthode d'Odlyzko-Poitou-Serre" —
i.e. exactly our consumer/producer split.]

Application: the flatness condition in `IsHardlyRamified` says
`ρ̄|_{G_{ℚ₃}}` comes from a finite flat commutative group scheme `J`
over `ℤ₃`; since `V` is a `k`-module and `char k = 3`, `J` is killed by
`3` — so `p = 3`, `n = 1`, `e = e(ℚ₃/ℚ₃) = 1`. With
`L = ℚ₃(J(ℚ̄₃))` the Corollaire gives
`v₀(𝔇_{L/ℚ₃}) < 1 + 1/(3−1) = 3/2`.
The completion `K_w` (`w | 3`) sits inside `L` (any `σ` fixing `L`
pointwise fixes the points of `J`, hence lies in `ker ρ̄`, hence fixes
`K`), and the different is monotone in towers
(`𝔇_{L/ℚ₃} = 𝔇_{L/K_w} · 𝔇_{K_w/ℚ₃}`), so
`v₀(𝔇_{K_w/ℚ₃}) < 3/2` as well. Since `K/ℚ` is Galois, the 3-part of
the root discriminant `rd(K) = |disc K|^{1/[K:ℚ]}` equals
`3^{v₀(𝔇_{K_w/ℚ₃})}`, hence is **strictly less than `3^(3/2)`**.
[DERIVED from the VERIFIED statement — the specialization
`p = 3, n = 1, e = 1` and the tower/Galois bookkeeping are standard but
are exactly the ~200 lines of connective tissue the hypothesis seat
flagged as unformalized.]

### 1.3 Ramification at 2 (tameness)

Hardly-ramified condition (3): `ρ̄|_{G_{ℚ₂}}` preserves a line with
unramified square-trivial quotient character; det = `χ̄₃` is unramified
at 2; hence both diagonal characters of the inertia-at-2 action are
trivial on inertia, i.e. `I₂` acts through **unipotent** upper-triangular
matrices. In characteristic 3 a nontrivial unipotent has order exactly 3
(`(g−1)² = 0 ⇒ g³ = 1`). Wild inertia at 2 is pro-2 and maps to this
3-group, hence acts trivially: **`K/ℚ` is tame at 2 with
`e₂ = |ρ̄(I₂)| ∈ {1, 3}`**. Tame ramification gives different exponent
`e₂ − 1` (Serre, Corps Locaux III §6, standard), so the 2-part of
`rd(K)` is `2^{(e₂−1)/e₂} ≤ 2^{2/3}`. [DERIVED — standard; no primary
web verification needed beyond textbook tameness, but note this is the
step that fixes the exponent `2/3` rather than the naive tame cap
`(e−1)/e → 1`.]

### 1.4 The bound

Unramified outside {2, 3}, so
`rd(K) < 2^(2/3) · 3^(3/2)` (strict, from the strict 3-part), i.e.

```
rd(K) < 2^(2/3) · 3^(3/2) = 8.248377821991616…
```

This is the consumer-side Fontaine upper bound. Against the producer
side: the axiom `Odlyzko_statement` gives `rd(K) ≥ 8.25` once `K` is
totally complex (§1.1) of degree ≥ 18 (§4) — contradiction, forcing
`ρ̄` reducible, which is `mod_three`.

## 2. The number 8.2484 — CONFIRMED

`U = 2^(2/3) · 3^(3/2)`. Exponent arithmetic:

- `3^(3/2) = 3·√3 = 5.196152422706632…`; `2^(2/3) = 1.587401051968199…`
- exponent origin: `3/2 = n + 1/(p−1) = 1 + 1/2` (Fontaine, p = 3,
  n = 1, e = 1); `2/3 = (e₂−1)/e₂` with `e₂ = 3` (tame at 2, unipotent
  inertia of order 3).
- `U² = 2^(4/3)·3³ = 54·2^(1/3) = 68.03573669432315`, `√ = U` ✓.
- `U = 8.248377821991616` (float64); `8.25 − U = 0.0016221780`
  (`0.019667 %`); Poitou table value at n = 18 is `9.305672`
  (`(9.3057 − U)/U = 12.8 %`). Matches the reconciled doc's §2.1 and the
  adjudication's certified margin arithmetic exactly.
  [VERIFIED — recomputed here; the constant's *derivation* is now
  sourced (§1), which is what R1 demanded. Note it is genuinely a
  different number from the repo's `2^(2/3)·3^(7/8) ≈ 4.1511`
  (`DiscriminantBounds.lean`), whose `7/8` is a *tame-at-3* exponent
  (`e₃ = 8`), not the flat/wild exponent `3/2` — see §4.]

**Verdict: `8.2484 = 2^(2/3)·3^(3/2)` is CORRECT for our situation**
(mod-3, group scheme killed by p¹, base ℚ so e = 1, tame-at-2 with
e₂ | 3). It is not folklore: every factor is forced by a hardly-ramified
axiom clause plus Fontaine's Corollaire.

## 3. Lean-ready proposition draft

Prose statement (blueprint-ready, for ch03/ch04 next to
`hardly_ramified_mod3_reducible`):

> **Proposition (consumer-side Fontaine bound).** Let `k` be a finite
> field of characteristic 3 and `ρ̄ : G_ℚ → GL₂(k)` hardly ramified and
> irreducible, and let `K = ℚ̄^{ker ρ̄}`. Then `K` is a totally complex
> number field, `[K : ℚ] ≥ 18`, and
> `|disc K| < (2^(2/3)·3^(3/2))^{[K:ℚ]}`. In particular
> `|disc K| < 8.25^{[K:ℚ]}`, contradicting Théorème A of [Fontaine
> 1985] combined with the Odlyzko–Poitou lower bound; hence no such
> irreducible `ρ̄` exists.

Suggested formal shape, matching `FLT/Assumptions/Odlyzko.lean`
conventions (real-cast discriminant, `rpow` on ℝ, hypotheses as
instances where possible). This would live in a new
`FLT/GaloisRepresentation/HardlyRamified/KernelField.lean` (or in
`ModThree.lean`), with `kernelField ρ` the (to-be-built) `IntermediateField`
of `ℚ̄`-points fixed by `ker ρ`:

## 4. The degree question: 18, and the 15–17 gap

**Why the axiom demands ≥ 18.** Purely producer-side: 18 is the degree
from which Poitou's GRH-free table certifies a root-discriminant lower
bound ≥ 8.25 (table p. 17: 9.305672 at n = 18; the closed form (16)
already fails to reach 8.2484 at n = 18, which is why the table entry is
the certified producer). The axiom's floor is where 8.25 becomes
available, not where the consumer field lands. [VERIFIED by the panel's
Numdam check, adjudication §"certified"; not re-verified here.]

**What degree the constructed field actually has.**
`[K:ℚ] = |im ρ̄|`, `im ρ̄ ⊆ GL₂(k)` irreducible with
`det(im ρ̄) = {±1}`. Dichotomy on `3 | |im ρ̄|`:

- **Wild/order-divisible-by-3 case.** An order-3 element of `GL₂(k)`
  (char 3) is unipotent (`(g−1)³ = g³ − 1 = 0`). An irreducible
  subgroup of `GL₂(k̄₃)` containing a nontrivial unipotent contains
  `SL₂(𝔽₃)` (Dickson's theorem), so `|im ρ̄| ≥ 24`; with det onto
  `{±1}` in fact ≥ 48 when `k = 𝔽₃`. **Degree ≥ 24 ≥ 18** — the axiom
  applies with room to spare, and this is the only case where the §1
  bound `8.2484` is actually needed. [DERIVED — Dickson is standard
  (e.g. Suzuki, Group Theory I, or Serre Prop. 15/16-style arguments);
  no Lean formalization exists in the repo.]

- **Tame-at-3 case (`3 ∤ |im ρ̄|`).** Then `ρ̄` is tame at 3 too;
  `ρ̄(I₃)` is cyclic of prime-to-3 order `e₃`, contained in a maximal
  torus, and for `k = 𝔽₃` the maximal prime-to-3 cyclic order in
  `GL₂(𝔽₃)` is 8 (nonsplit torus `𝔽₉^×`), so `e₃ ≤ 8` and
  `rd(K) ≤ 2^(2/3) · 3^(7/8) = 4.1511…` — **exactly the repo's other
  constant** (`rootDiscrBound_thirteen_lt`/`_fourteen_gt` threshold in
  `FLT/NumberField/DiscriminantBounds.lean`). Minkowski
  (`rootDiscrBound` is strictly monotone; `rootDiscrBound 14 ≈ 4.211 >
  4.1511 > rootDiscrBound 13 ≈ 4.132`) then forces **degree ≤ 13**
  (`le_fourteen_of_rootDiscrBound` gives ≤ 14; the strict `fourteen_gt`
  comparison actually excludes 14 as well). For general finite `k`,
  `e₃` can exceed 8 (`|𝔽_{9^r}^×|`), but then `|im ρ̄| ≥ 2e₃ ≥ 20`
  (irreducible-nonabelian image versus the torus normalizer) while the
  tame cap only degrades to `2^(2/3)·3^(1−1/e₃) < 2^(2/3)·3 = 4.762`,
  and `rootDiscrBound n` passes 4.60 by n = 21 and 4.76 by n = 27 —
  closable by the same monotonicity lemma, but this sub-case needs its
  own short argument. [DERIVED; the group theory is unformalized. Note
  the residual degree-≤13 tame outcome is not a discriminant
  contradiction at all — it must be killed by the small-image/dihedral
  classification (conductor-2 class-field-theory arguments), which is a
  separate consumer obligation outside R1's scope; flagged, not
  solved.]

**Can 15–17 arise? No.** Wild case ⇒ degree ≥ 24; tame case ⇒ degree
≤ 13 (via the `3^(7/8)` threshold) or ≥ 20 (large-`e₃` sub-case).
Degrees 14–17 are unreachable for the constructed field, and 15–17 in
particular are covered by the **conjunction** of (a) Dickson (wild ⇒
≥ 24) and (b) the already-formalized Minkowski threshold
`rootDiscrBound_lt_iff_lt_fourteen` applied under the tame-at-3 case
split. The panel's "nothing covers 15–17" finding is therefore repaired
at the *mathematical* level by this case split; at the *formal* level
items (a) and the case split itself are new proof obligations, and the
adjudication's warning stands: a degree-19 axiom shortcut would still
be strictly worse, since it would additionally demand covering 18–18
via (a) with no compensating gain.
